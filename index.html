World Cup 2026 — schedule info & FAQ

matchcal26 is a free World Cup 2026 calendar that shows every fixture in your own local timezone. Pick your team to see their matches, check the win predictions, and add games to your Google or Apple Calendar in one tap — no app and no sign-up. The tournament runs from 11 June to 19 July 2026, co-hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico, with 48 teams playing 104 matches across 16 host cities.

When does the FIFA World Cup 2026 start?

The 2026 World Cup starts on 11 June 2026 with the opening match in Mexico City, and runs until the final on 19 July 2026 at MetLife Stadium near New York.

Where is the 2026 World Cup being held?

It is co-hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico across 16 host cities — the first 48-team World Cup, with 104 matches in total.

How many teams are in the 2026 World Cup?

48 teams, drawn into 12 groups of four. The top two from each group plus the eight best third-placed teams advance to a new Round of 32.

What time does my team play in my timezone?

Pick your team above and every kick-off is converted automatically to your local timezone. You can add any match — or your team's whole group stage — to Google or Apple Calendar in one tap.

How is the win probability calculated?

Each match shows the implied chance of a home win, draw or away win. These come from bookmakers' decimal odds (probability = 1 ÷ odds), normalised to remove the bookmaker margin so the three add up to 100%. It reflects the betting market's view — not a guarantee.

Is matchcal26 free and official?

Yes, it's completely free with no sign-up. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FIFA.
